# Module asset provenance

All code, shaders, palette choices and collision-free referee placeholder in this
module are original work for this project (2026-10-02), licensed under MIT; see LICENSE.

audio/assets contains 34 original procedurally synthesised mono 24 kHz WAV files.
They use fixed-seed mathematical oscillators and filtered noise, with no recordings,
third-party samples, soundfonts, copyrighted melodies or downloaded audio.
They are dedicated under CC0 1.0; see audio/assets/LICENSE.txt and audio_manifest.json.
Reproducible generator and objective checks are in the delivery root's tools directory.

No external textures, models, logos or commercial materials are embedded in this module.
The demo-only baseline ball geometry is outside this module and retains its separate MIT
notice in the delivery root. Do not copy demo files when integrating the module.

Rules reference IFAB 2026/27 official pages, reviewed 2026-10-02:
https://www.theifab.com/laws-of-the-game-documents/
No IFAB text pages, diagrams or logo assets are bundled.
